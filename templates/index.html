Helpful Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding admissions, routine schedules, hostel arrangements, and faculty appointments.

What is the admission procedure for new students?

Parents or guardians can apply online by clicking "Apply for Admission" on our homepage. Complete the Admission Form, upload the required documents (Aadhaar Card, Birth Certificate, and Photos), and submit the application. Once reviewed and approved by the administration, you will receive confirmation with class assignment and registration code.

What are the class timings and school routing?

Al-Hidayet Mission runs classes from Nursery up to Class Ten. Normal school hours are Monday to Thursday and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 11:30 AM, and Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM. Coaching Classes run from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM on school days. Schedules are viewable inside the Student and Teacher dashboards.

Is there a hostel facility at this branch?

At this branch, day-hostel facilities are available, which provide supervised study hours, moral education, learning reinforcement, and nutritious meals for all day-boarders.

How do I pay school and coaching fees?

Fees are collected directly in cash or via authorized bank account transfer at the school administration office. Computer-generated official fee receipts are issued instantly for all records.

How can qualified teachers join Al-Hidayet Mission?

Passionate educators are welcomed to apply directly through our "Application Portal" under the "Teacher Recruitment" section. Provide academic credentials, experience, and subject preferences for prompt administrative review.
